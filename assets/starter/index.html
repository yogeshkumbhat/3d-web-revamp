Why this shell exists

Most 3D sites are built scene-first and retrofit the fallback afterwards, which is why the fallback is usually a blank rectangle. Building in this order — content, poster, then scene — makes the degraded path impossible to forget.

What to replace

The torus knot in the scene module is a placeholder. Swap in the actual product or artefact, run it through the asset pipeline, and regenerate the poster from the finished scene so the two never drift apart.
